# Hostel Regulations (2026)

## 1. Timings

1.1 Residents of the boys hostel must be inside the premises by 11:00 PM.

1.2 Residents of the girls hostel must be inside the premises by 10:00 PM.

1.3 A resident who will return after curfew must obtain written permission from the warden before 6:00 PM on the same day.

## 2. Charges

2.1 The hostel fee is Rs. 45,000 per semester, paid with the first tuition instalment.

2.2 A refundable security deposit of Rs. 10,000 is paid once at the time of admission to the hostel.

2.3 Mess charges are Rs. 18,000 per month and are billed separately from the hostel fee.

## 3. Visitors

3.1 Visitors are allowed in the hostel lounge only, between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM, and must leave their CNIC at the gate.

3.2 A resident may request a change of room within the first 2 weeks of the semester.
