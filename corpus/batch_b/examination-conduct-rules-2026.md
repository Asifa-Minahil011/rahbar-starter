# Examination Conduct Rules (2026)

## 1. Before the paper

1.1 A student must reach the examination hall 15 minutes before the paper starts and must carry the university ID card.

1.2 A student arriving more than 20 minutes after the start of the paper is not allowed to enter the hall.

## 2. During the paper

2.1 Mobile phones, smart watches and any other electronic device are not allowed in the hall.

2.2 A student may ask for an additional answer sheet from the invigilator and must write the roll number on every sheet.

## 3. Unfair means

3.1 A student found using unfair means is awarded zero marks in that paper and is referred to the Disciplinary Committee.

3.2 A student who helps another student to use unfair means is dealt with in the same way.
