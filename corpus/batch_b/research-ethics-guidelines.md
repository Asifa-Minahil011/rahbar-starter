# Research Ethics Guidelines

## 1. Human subjects

1.1 Any study that collects data from human participants must be approved by the Institutional Review Board before data collection starts.

1.2 Participants must give informed consent in writing and may withdraw at any time without giving a reason.

## 2. Integrity

2.1 A thesis or research paper must have a similarity index below 20 percent, excluding the bibliography.

2.2 Raw research data must be retained for 5 years after the work is published.
