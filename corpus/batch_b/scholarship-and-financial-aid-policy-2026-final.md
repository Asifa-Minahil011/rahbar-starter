# Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy (2026)

## 1. Merit scholarships

1.1 A student with a semester GPA of 3.50 or higher receives a 50 percent waiver of tuition fee for the next semester.

1.2 A student with a semester GPA of 3.75 or higher receives a 100 percent waiver of tuition fee for the next semester.

1.3 A merit scholarship is withdrawn if the cumulative GPA falls below 3.00 or if the student is found guilty of a disciplinary offence.

## 2. Need-based aid

2.1 Need-based aid is awarded on the basis of an income certificate and the last two utility bills of the family.

2.2 An application for need-based aid must reach the Financial Aid Office within 15 days of the start of the semester.

## 3. Instalments

3.1 A student not receiving a waiver may pay the tuition fee in 3 equal instalments, due in the 1st, 6th and 11th week of the semester.

3.2 A late instalment carries a surcharge of Rs. 1,000 for each week of delay.
