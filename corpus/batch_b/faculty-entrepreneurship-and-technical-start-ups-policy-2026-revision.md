# Faculty Entrepreneurship and Technical Start-ups Policy (2026 revision)

This revision replaces the 2022 edition of the policy.

## 1. Participation

1.1 A faculty member may hold up to 20 percent equity in a technical start-up while remaining in full-time service.

1.2 A faculty member must disclose any start-up involvement to the Office of Research, Innovation and Commercialization within 30 days.

## 2. Use of university resources

2.1 A start-up may use university laboratories for up to 12 months against a monthly usage charge approved by the Dean.

2.2 Intellectual property created with university funds is shared between the inventor and the university in the ratio of 60 to 40.
