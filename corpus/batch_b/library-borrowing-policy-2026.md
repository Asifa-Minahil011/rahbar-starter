# Central Library Borrowing and Fines Policy (2026)

## 1. Borrowing

1.1 An undergraduate student may borrow up to 4 books at a time for a loan period of 14 days.

1.2 A graduate student may borrow up to 6 books at a time for a loan period of 21 days.

1.3 A loan may be renewed once for 7 days, provided no other member has reserved the book.

1.4 Reference books, theses and past examination papers are for use inside the library and are not issued.

## 2. Fines and losses

2.1 An overdue book is fined at Rs. 10 per day per book.

2.2 A member who loses a book pays its replacement cost and a processing fee of Rs. 500.

2.3 A lost library card is replaced on payment of Rs. 300.

## 3. Membership

3.1 Library membership ends on the last day of the semester in which the student graduates or withdraws, and all dues must be cleared before the clearance certificate is issued.
